Feature guide

A complete
production surface.

Direct Link 2 hardware control and Linux-native processing live in one application. Anything processed locally appears consistently in preview, capture, recording, and the optional virtual camera.

Camera and image

Use the mode bar for Normal, AI Tracking, Whiteboard, Overhead, and DeskView. The View and Image pages expose pan, tilt, center, zoom, autofocus/manual focus, mirror, privacy, HDR, automatic or manual exposure, white balance, anti-flicker, and the full standard color set.

Cooperative control

Link Studio never detaches uvcvideo. Camera changes are serialized and paced so the preview, OBS, and other Linux clients can coexist safely.

Tracking and composition

Choose camera AI for the Link 2's native single-person tracking or a Link Studio engine for local single/group tracking. Local modes support one tracking boundary and six pause areas drawn directly over preview. Framing options cover head, half body, and whole body.

MediaPipe models are bundled and loaded lazily. No camera frame is sent to an inference service.

Audio and capture

The Audio page controls the Link 2's hardware Voice Focus, Voice Suppression, and Music Balance modes. PipeWire supplies source choice, mute, and volume. Standard recordings combine H.264 video with 48 kHz mono AAC from the selected microphone.

Scenes and color templates

Scenes save a complete camera setup and support apply, update, rename, delete, and default-on-startup. Independent color templates recall exposure, white balance, and color values without changing composition or smart mode.

Remote, shortcuts, and tools

  • Phone remote: opt-in LAN server with an unguessable session token and QR pairing.
  • Global actions: fourteen compositor-managed actions through the XDG portal; no Hyprland file edits.
  • Teleprompter: local scripts, import/edit, speed, font size, colors, opacity, guide, looping, and countdown.
  • Local AI recording: WAV voice notes, pause/resume, timestamped notes and screenshot markers, optional Whisper transcription, and Markdown summary.
  • Support bundle: diagnostics, formats, runtime state, and rotating logs without camera images.

Omarchy theming

Link Studio watches the active colors.toml and theme name. Libadwaita surfaces, accents, selections, semantic colors, and the phone interface update immediately after an Omarchy theme change.
