Quick start

Connect the Link 2 directly over USB, launch Link Studio, and the first compatible camera is selected automatically. The preview opens using the camera's advertised 1280×720, 30 fps mode.

link-studio

The eight control pages separate scenes, physical view, image controls, smart modes, software effects, audio, production tools, and device/application settings.

PresetsSave ten scenes and choose one to apply at startup.
ViewGimbal, zoom, focus, orientation, and privacy.
ImageHDR, exposure, white balance, color, and templates.
SmartAI modes, composition, speed, groups, and areas.
EffectsLocal AI processing and virtual-camera output.
AudioLink 2 DSP modes, source, mute, and volume.
